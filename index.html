Laser correction methods

Lenticule-based (ReLEx SMILE)
  • correction within the corneal tissue without creating a flap;
  • recovery within 1-2 days;
  • A special laser creates a tiny lens inside the cornea through a smile-shaped incision; the surgeon removes it.
Flap-based (LASIK, Femto LASIK, Femto Super LASIK)
  • correction beneath a corneal flap;
  • recovery within 5-7 days;
  • a femtosecond laser or keratome creates the flap; an excimer laser reshapes the cornea beneath it.
Surface ablation (PRK)
  • reshaping the corneal surface;
  • recovery within 1-3 months;
  • performed with an excimer laser.

Benefits of the SMILE technique for the patient

High accuracy
One of the most advanced laser vision correction methods, with predictable outcomes: 20/20 vision or better in 93.4% of cases.
Fast recovery
The patient can return to their normal lifestyle with restored vision within a few hours.
Safety of the procedure
The incision is just 2–4 mm, compared with larger incisions used by other methods. The procedure also avoids the risk of flap displacement after surgery.

Stages of treatment

1/3
Before surgery
  • Complete a comprehensive diagnostic test;
  • Take the necessary tests and make sure there are no contraindications;
  • Stop using soft lenses several days before surgery;
  • Use eye drops recommended by your ophthalmologist.

Stages of treatment

2/3
During surgery
Preparation
Anesthetic drops are used that completely eliminate pain. An eyelid dilator is used to prevent involuntary blinking.
Lenticule formation
A femtosecond laser creates a special microlens in the cornea. At the same time, a smile-shaped micro-incision measuring 2–4 mm is formed.
Lenticule extraction
An experienced ophthalmologist carefully removes the formed lenticule through a previously created micro-incision.

Stages of treatment

3/3
Final stage
After the lenticule is removed, a natural change occurs in the cornea. It adapts to the new shape, providing improved visual function.
After surgery
  • Use prescribed eye drops;
  • Wear sunglasses when outdoors if necessary.

Contraindications:

Progressive myopia and farsightedness
Severe eye diseases (glaucoma, cataracts, retinal detachment)
Suspicion of keratoconus or its presence
Pregnancy, lactation period during the first 3 months
Autoimmune uncompensated diseases (uveitis, SLE, thyroiditis, inflammation of the eye and its appendages in the active stage)
