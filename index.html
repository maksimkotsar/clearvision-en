Laser correction methods

Lenticular (ReLEx SMILE)
  • correction in the thickness of the cornea without cutting a flap;
  • recovery within 1-2 days;

Valve (LASIK, Femto LASIK, Femto Super LASIK)
  • correction of the cornea under the flap;
  • recovery within 5-7 days;
  • performed with a femtosecond laser or keratome, an excimer laser is used to polish the cornea under the valve.
Superficial (FRK, PRK)
  • grinding the surface of the cornea;
  • recovery within 1-3 months;
  • performed with an excimer laser.
